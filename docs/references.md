# Technical references

These sources explain product behavior. They do not verify this lab's deployment. Match documentation to installed versions before applying settings.

- [OPNsense firewall rules](https://docs.opnsense.org/manual/firewall.html) — stateful policy and rule fields.
- [OPNsense NAT](https://docs.opnsense.org/manual/nat.html) — translation and inbound/outbound distinctions.
- [OPNsense aliases](https://docs.opnsense.org/manual/aliases.html) — alias handling and table contents.
- [OPNsense firewall diagnostics](https://docs.opnsense.org/manual/diagnostics_firewall.html) — inspecting rules and alias membership.
- [OPNsense dnsmasq](https://docs.opnsense.org/manual/dnsmasq.html) — DHCP service configuration.
- [OPNsense Suricata](https://docs.opnsense.org/manual/ips.html) — IDS/IPS distinctions, capture placement, ET Open and EVE syslog output.
- [Proxmox VE administration guide](https://pve.proxmox.com/pve-docs/pve-admin-guide.pdf) — bridges, VLAN awareness and guest networking.
- [Wazuh documentation](https://documentation.wazuh.com/current/) — consult the selected release during the future deployment phase.

Product names belong to their respective projects. This portfolio is an independent learning project.
